# The Golden Hour in Workplace Safety

When a worker suffers a fall on an isolated part of a massive warehouse floor, every second counts. The "Golden Hour" refers to the critical window following a traumatic injury where prompt medical treatment has the highest likelihood of preventing death.

## The Challenge

A top-tier logistics provider operating a 1-million-square-foot distribution center faced a serious issue: isolated slips, trips, and falls were going unnoticed for up to 20 minutes in low-traffic aisles.

## The Solution

The company deployed **Argus AI Fall Detection** across their existing 120-camera network. Using advanced pose-estimation neural networks, Argus was able to distinguish between:
* A worker bending down to pick up a box
* A worker sitting down to rest
* A worker experiencing a sudden, uncontrolled fall

## The Results

Within three months of deployment:
* **Detection Time:** Dropped from an average of 14 minutes to **under 5 seconds**.
* **False Positives:** Maintained at less than 1 per week due to Argus's sophisticated temporal analysis.
* **Medical Response:** Emergency teams were dispatched 75% faster, significantly improving worker outcomes.

The ROI of safety is immeasurable when a life is on the line.
